- The Office view has a third built-in style, **Cozy**, next to Desks and Studio in the Style pickers: the same zones scene as Studio — each instance walks to the area for its state (work desks, the lounge's sofa and coffee, the beanbags, the whiteboard) — in a warm pixel-art office drawn as one picture that ships with AgEnD, with the same little people. Its notice board says a skin pack can restyle the room. Cozy is the default for a browser that has not picked a style and for a new office in Edit offices; a saved office keeps its own (one saved without a style stays Desks). An uploaded pack can no longer be named `cozy`. (#2631)
