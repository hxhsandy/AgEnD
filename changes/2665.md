- Office **Edit layout** can place a furniture item's night place. Every item with a bedtime `night` has a night handle, "Item {id} (night)": its night picture drawn faint, a moon, and a thin line to its day place. An item that sleeps in place shows it on its day place, and it follows the item there; drag it away to give the item its own night place, or use **Back to the pack's night place** to undo it. It is saved with the office's other overrides as `furnitureNight` (an item the pack has with a `night`, on the canvas). At bedtime the item sleeps at that place, else the pack's night place, else its day place. On disk the host keeps night places beside the offices, so a host rolled back to an earlier 2.3.2 beta still reads every office; only the night places are missing there. (#2665)
