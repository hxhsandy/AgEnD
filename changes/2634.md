- Office skin packs can ask for straight walking with a new optional top-level `"straight": true` (checked by the page and the host; default false, and false for the built-in Studio). Every walk on such a pack — to a seat, an idle stroll, picking up mail, an NPC's stroll, an agent's `go` — turns each diagonal leg into an L, across first and then up or down, at the usual walking speed. A character set's three walking strips (`walkSide`, `walkDown`, `walkUp`) then always match the way it moves. Lay the pack's graph out with horizontal and vertical edges and the corners land on the aisles. Packs without it walk as before. (#2634)
