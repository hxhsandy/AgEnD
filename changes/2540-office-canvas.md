section: Changed
---
- The Office view fills the Fleet panel and pans and zooms like the org chart's Canvas: drag to pan, the wheel or a pinch to zoom, **+**/**−**/**Fit** buttons or the `+ - 0` keys, and an overview to jump (not on a phone). Zoom snaps to pixel levels (each logical unit a whole number of screen pixels, drawn unsmoothed) so pixel art stays crisp; Fit takes the largest level that shows the whole scene, the exact fit only when that would be tiny. The view is kept per device for each office and style. Around the 16:9 scene is a matte — a skin pack's new optional `matte` field (`#rrggbb`, checked by the page and the host), else the background's edge colour, else the dashboard's background. Hover, click, name tags, bubbles, crowd counts and **Edit layout** work at any zoom (a drag on a handle moves it, anywhere else pans).
- Skin packs (format v1) gain an optional `foreground`: a 16:9 PNG over the whole zones scene, drawn above the people, furniture and items in the air but below name tags, bubbles and crowd counts — for door frames, pillars and table fronts that hide whoever walks behind them. A furniture item's `depth` may now be `"y"`, drawing it among the people by base y (whoever stands lower is in front). Both are checked by the page and the host.
- The Office view has a **Fullscreen** mode (a button under **Fit**, or `f`; Esc leaves): only the office shows — no dashboard header, tabs or bars — with the zoom buttons, the overview (not on a phone), a compact office switcher and Exit floating over it. It uses the browser's fullscreen; where a page cannot have it (iPhone Safari) or the browser refuses, the office covers the window instead, clear of the safe areas. Pan, zoom, hover, click-to-chat (which leaves fullscreen first) and live updates keep working; nothing about it is remembered.
